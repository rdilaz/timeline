# Timeline: 3-minute demo, shot by shot

Target length 2:55, hard limit 3:00. Record the laptop screen (Chrome or Edge, full screen) plus one short phone clip.

The video opens on the plain Basic template so anyone gets it in seconds. Ink scroll and Cosmos come later as the reveal.

## Before you press record

- Open the live site a minute early: the free host takes about 30 seconds to wake.
- Make sure the site opens on Basic. If not: Templates, Basic.
- Load Llama once: Make yours, Your words, Llama 3.2, Try a made-up example, Write my timeline. The first run downloads about 900 MB. After that it loads from the browser cache in seconds.
- Rehearse the Wi-Fi-off shot once. If Llama errors with Wi-Fi off, keep Wi-Fi on and drop that line.
- Reset to the example before the take: clear site data for the page, then reload.
- Have the story below ready to paste.

Story to paste (made up, with a phone number on purpose):

> I grew up in a small town near the sea and got a secondhand piano at nine. Call me at 555-201-3344. I studied engineering, then moved to a new city for a design job. Last spring I opened a tiny online shop for my bowls. Next I want to walk the Kumano Kodo.

## Shots

Final script, with Muse's (Marsha's) tightened lines. Every shot uses something the app really does. No intro card, no greeting.

## If something breaks while filming

- Llama won't load: film the Muse shot as the AI option, and say Llama runs on devices with WebGPU.
- Muse is off: skip 1:30 to 1:40 and give the time to Cosmos.
- The site is asleep: refresh once and wait 30 seconds.
- Short on time: cut Futures from the Ink shot first, then the phone clip.
